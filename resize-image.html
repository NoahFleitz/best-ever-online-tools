How to use Resize Image (free, no upload)

  1. Drop one or more images.
  2. Choose Percentage (for example 50% for half size) or Pixels.
  3. With pixels, enter a width, a height, or both. With aspect ratio on, images fit inside the box without stretching.
  4. Click Resize images and download.

Frequently asked questions

Will resizing make my image blurry?

Making images smaller keeps them sharp. Making them much larger than the original can't add detail, so they'll look soft.

Can I resize many images to the same width?

Yes. Choose Pixels, enter only a width, and every image is scaled to that width with its own height.

What format do I get back?

The same type you started with: PNG stays PNG, JPG stays JPG, WebP stays WebP.

Is it private?

Yes. Your images are processed in your browser and never uploaded.
