About Best Ever Online Tools

Best Ever Online Tools is a free collection of 19 video, image, PDF and text tools that run entirely in your web browser. Modern browsers can process video, images and documents on their own, so there's no need to upload your files to someone else's server and wait.

That also means there are no costs per conversion, which is why there are no daily limits, file size caps or paid tiers.

All tools

Video tools

  • Video to Short: Make landscape videos vertical (9:16) with blur fill or crop.
  • Trim Video: Cut a clip out of any video at the exact start and end you choose.
  • Compress Video: Shrink a video to 10 MB, 25 MB or any size you need.
  • Video to GIF: Turn a short video clip into an animated GIF.
  • Extract Audio: Save a video's sound as MP3, WAV or M4A.

Image tools

  • Compress Image: Shrink JPG, PNG and WebP files, many at a time.
  • Resize Image: Change image dimensions by pixels or percent.
  • Convert Image: Turn WebP, PNG, GIF and more into JPG, PNG or WebP.
  • Crop Image: Cut a photo to any area or aspect ratio.
  • Remove Metadata: Strip GPS location and EXIF data from photos.

PDF tools

  • Merge PDF: Combine several PDFs into one file.
  • Split PDF: Split a PDF or pull out the pages you need.
  • Images to PDF: Turn JPG and PNG images into one PDF.
  • PDF to Images: Save PDF pages as PNG or JPG images.
  • Rotate PDF: Turn sideways pages the right way up.

Text & color tools

  • QR Code Generator: Make QR codes for links, Wi-Fi and text.
  • Word Counter: Count words, characters and reading time.
  • Case Converter: Switch text between UPPER, lower, Title, camelCase…
  • Color Picker: Get HEX/RGB codes and pick colors from images.
