Frequently asked questions

What is Best Ever Online Tools?

Best Ever Online Tools is a free collection of 19 tools for video, images, PDFs and text, such as converting video to YouTube Shorts format, compressing images, merging PDFs and making QR codes. Every tool runs inside your web browser.

Is it really free?

Yes. Every tool is free with no daily limits, no file size caps, no sign-up and no watermark. The site is supported by ads.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Your browser does all the processing on your own device, so your videos, photos and documents never leave your computer or phone.

Do I need to install anything?

No. The tools work in any modern browser on Windows, Mac, Linux, Android and iPhone. You can also install the site as an app from your browser menu.

Which browsers work best?

Recent versions of Chrome and Edge are fastest, especially for video. Firefox and Safari also work.
