    Frequently asked questions

    Does it save my text?

    Your text is kept only in this browser so it's still there if you reload. It's never sent anywhere.

    How are words counted?

    Any run of letters or numbers counts as a word, including words with apostrophes and hyphens such as don't and well-known.

    What's a good length for a tweet, meta description or essay?

    X/Twitter allows 280 characters, meta descriptions show about 155–160 characters, and a typical college essay runs 500–650 words.
