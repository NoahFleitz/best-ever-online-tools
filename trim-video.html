How to use Trim Video (free, no upload)

  1. Choose or drop your video.
  2. Play it and press Now next to Start and End at the moments you want, or type the times in seconds.
  3. Click Preview clip to watch exactly the part you'll get, before anything is processed.
  4. Pick Same as original for full quality or Smaller file to save space.
  5. Click Trim video and download the MP4.

Frequently asked questions

Does trimming lower the quality?

The clip is re-encoded at the original resolution and frame rate with a high bitrate, so it looks the same to the eye. Choose Smaller file if you'd rather save space.

Is there a length or size limit?

No hard limit. Very long or 4K videos take longer and use more memory, so for huge files trim a shorter section.

Is my video uploaded?

No. Everything happens inside your browser on your own device.

Why is the output MP4 even if I uploaded a MOV or WebM?

MP4 plays almost everywhere, so it's the safest format to download.
