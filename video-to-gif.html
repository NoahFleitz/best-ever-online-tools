How to use Video to GIF (free, no upload)

  1. Choose or drop your video.
  2. Mark the start and end. Keep it short: GIFs get big fast.
  3. Pick a width and frame rate. 480 px at 15 fps works well for chats and social posts.
  4. Click Make GIF and download it.

Frequently asked questions

How long can my GIF be?

There's no hard limit, but GIFs are much larger than videos. Under 10 seconds at 480 px is a good rule of thumb.

How do I make the GIF smaller?

Use a lower width, a lower frame rate, or a shorter clip.

Is there a watermark?

No.

Is my video uploaded?

No. The GIF is made in your browser on your device.
