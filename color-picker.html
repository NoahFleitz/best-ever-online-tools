How to use Color Picker (free, no upload)

  1. Pick a color with the color box, type a HEX code, or click Pick from screen (Chrome and Edge).
  2. Or drop an image and click on it to grab any color. Zoom in to pick a single exact pixel. The main colors of the image appear as a palette.
  3. Click Copy next to the format you need.

Frequently asked questions

What do AA and AAA mean in the contrast check?

They're accessibility levels from the WCAG guidelines. AA needs a contrast of at least 4.5:1 for normal text, AAA needs 7:1.

Why is the CMYK value different from my print software?

This is a simple conversion without a printer color profile. For exact print colors, use your printer's profile.

Is my image uploaded?

No. Colors are read from the image in your browser.
