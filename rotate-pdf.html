How to use Rotate PDF (free, no upload)

  1. Choose a PDF.
  2. Click a page to rotate it 90° clockwise. Click again to keep turning.
  3. Use Rotate all to turn every page at once.
  4. Click Save rotated PDF and download.

Frequently asked questions

Is the rotation permanent?

Yes. The saved PDF opens with the pages turned in every viewer.

Does it change the content or quality?

No. Only each page's rotation setting changes.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. It's all done in your browser.
