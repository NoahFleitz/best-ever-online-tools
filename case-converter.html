Frequently asked questions

How does Title Case work?

Major words are capitalized and short words like 'and', 'of' and 'the' stay lowercase unless they start the title.

What are camelCase and snake_case for?

They're naming styles in programming: camelCase joins words with capitals (myVariableName), snake_case joins them with underscores (my_variable_name).

Is my text sent anywhere?

No. Everything happens in your browser.
