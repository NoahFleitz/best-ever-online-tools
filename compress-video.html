How to use Compress Video (free, no upload)

  1. Choose or drop your video.
  2. Pick a target size. 10 MB fits Discord's free limit.
  3. Lower the resolution if the estimate says quality will be low. 720p is a good balance for phones.
  4. Optionally trim the video. Shorter clips look better at the same size.
  5. Click Compress video and download the MP4.

Frequently asked questions

Will the file be exactly the size I picked?

It aims a little under your target. Very simple or very busy footage can land slightly off, so pick a size with some room if the limit is strict.

Why does my compressed video look blurry?

The target size is small for the length and resolution. Lower the resolution, trim the clip, or choose a bigger size.

How do I send a video on Discord without Nitro?

Compress it to 10 MB here, then upload the MP4 to Discord.

Is my video uploaded?

No. The compression runs entirely in your browser.
