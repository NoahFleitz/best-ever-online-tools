Frequently asked questions

Do these QR codes expire?

No. The information is stored in the code itself, not on a server, so it works forever.

Can I track scans?

No. These are static codes with no tracking. That also means no one tracks the people who scan them.

What does error correction do?

Higher levels (Q, H) let the code scan even if part of it is damaged or covered, at the cost of a denser pattern.

How does the Wi-Fi QR code work?

Phones that scan it offer to join the network automatically, without typing the password.
